## 3. Full Descriptions

### English
**BabyDaily — The smart, shared daily routine tracker for modern parents & caregivers.**

Caring for your baby is a team effort. BabyDaily keeps parents, partners, babysitters, and grandparents synchronized in real-time, whether tracking the last feeding, monitoring naps, recording diapers, or logging medicine.

**Key Features:**
- 🍼 **Feedings Tracker**: Record breast milk (left/right duration), bottle amounts (ml/oz), or solids with one tap.
- 😴 **Nap & Night Sleep**: Start sleep timer, log wake-ups, and track daily total sleep.
- 🧷 **Diaper Changes**: Keep count of wet, dirty, and mixed diapers.
- 💊 **Medicine & Vitamins**: Log dosages, Vitamin D, and treatments on time.
- 📏 **Growth & Notes**: Record weight, height, temperature, and milestones.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Instant Family Sync**: Invite your partner, nanny, or family by email or phone. Auto-syncs across iOS, Android, and Web.
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders**: Set recurring alerts (e.g., feed every 3 hours after last feeding) or daily time-based reminders.
- 📊 **PDF & Excel Reports**: Export clean, printable pediatric reports for your doctor visits.
- 🌓 **Day & Night Themes**: Stunning soft-tinted palette with an automatic dark mode for low-light night feedings.
- 🌐 **Bilingual & RTL**: Full support for English and Hebrew (עברית).
